Daring Kangaroo and Daisy

Daring Kangaroo is a brave superhero with powerful jumping abilities, and Daisy is the helpful sidekick who uses plant powers to assist the team.

Foes

  • Dark Shadow
    • Minor Power: Can disappear in the dark.
    • Major Power: Can create powerful shadow storms.
  • Thunder King
    • Minor Power: Can create small sparks.
    • Major Power: Can control powerful lightning storms.
