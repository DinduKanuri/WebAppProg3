How to Make Tea

  1. Boil the water.
    1. Fill the kettle with water.
    2. Turn on the kettle and let the water boil.
  2. Place a tea bag in a cup.
  3. Pour the hot water into the cup.
  4. Let the tea steep for a few minutes.
  5. Add milk or sugar if desired.
